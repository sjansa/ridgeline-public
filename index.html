    Route options

    Routes follow the mapped runs and lifts, fastest first. Where the map leaves a gap, a short traverse over gentle ground is suggested and marked; check it with your own eyes. Off the route by more than 50 m, it plans again from where you are.

    Search for a place, or use your current location. Needs a connection.

    Line the overlay up by hand: drag to slide it, twist two fingers to roll it, pinch to zoom it. With a mouse, Shift-drag rolls and the wheel zooms. In camera mode a pinch (or the zoom buttons) zooms the camera and the overlay follows; choose another lens under Menu > View. (In the demo a drag looks around and a pinch zooms the photo.) Green circle, blue square and black diamond follow North American trail ratings; with run lines on, black runs are drawn in red and lifts in purple.

    Beta feedback

    Opens a new message in your mail app for you to send. It includes the readout below, with your position if the app has one, so the problem can be reproduced; you can delete that from the message. A live snapshot captures the view without this menu, the plain picture behind it, and the app's settings and readings at that moment.

    For diagnosing compass trouble: tap, then point at one landmark and slowly tip the phone from looking down to looking up and back. Works in camera mode.

    Safety and legal

    This app is a guide to what you are looking at, not a safety device. Run and lift positions, names and ratings come from a volunteer-made map and can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. The overlay can also be misaligned with the view.

    The app does not know which runs or lifts are open, where the ski-area boundary is, or anything about snow, weather, avalanche danger, cliffs or other hazards. Always obey signs, ropes, closures and ski patrol, and use the resort's official trail map. Terrain marked as backcountry is not patrolled or controlled.

    Do not use your phone while skiing or riding. Stop in a safe place, out of the way of others, before looking at the screen.

    Skiing and snowboarding are dangerous. You are responsible for your own decisions and safety. The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, and to the fullest extent the law allows its makers are not liable for any injury, loss or damage arising from its use.

    Privacy: camera pictures are processed on your phone and are never uploaded. Your location is used on your phone to choose a map and place the overlay. The map for setting a photo's spot loads satellite or topographic pictures of the area on screen from Esri or OpenTopoMap. When you download a map area, the outline of that area (and any place name you search for) is sent to the map servers that supply the data.

    Ridgeline is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any ski resort. Resort, lift and run names belong to their owners and are used only to identify them.
